About Little Lemon Restaurant

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist. The chefs draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and have a menu of 12–15 items that they rotate seasonally.

About Owners

Little Lemon is owned by two Italian brothers, Mario and Adrian, who moved to the United States to pursue their shared dream of owning a restaurant. Mario provides authentic dishes from his experience as a chef in Italy. Adrian helps in incorporatin the addditional cuisines from the Mediterranian region
